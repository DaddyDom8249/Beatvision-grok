# BeatVision Product Guard

BeatVision is not a generic AI app builder.

Its purpose is:

"Every Song Has a World. BeatVision Reveals It."

The song is the center of the product.

Before adding a feature, determine whether it directly improves:

1. understanding the song
2. revealing its visual world
3. maintaining visual continuity
4. storyboarding the song
5. generating scenes
6. animating scenes
7. assembling the final music video

If it does not support one of those goals, do not add it without explicit approval.

## Protect these concepts

- Song-first creation
- Visual World Report
- Creator approval
- Style Bible
- Character continuity
- Environment continuity
- Song master timeline
- Scene continuity
- Provider abstraction
- Real generation
- Final music video

Do not replace these with generic:

- AI chat
- prompt playgrounds
- social feeds
- generic project dashboards
- unrelated collaboration systems
- arbitrary gamification

Do not add features simply because they are easy to implement.

Preserve BeatVision's identity.
